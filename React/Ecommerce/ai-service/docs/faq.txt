Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How do I place an order?
A: Browse products, add them to your cart, and complete the checkout process.

Q: Do I need an account to place an order?
A: Yes, customers must create an account before placing an order.

Q: Can I order multiple products at once?
A: Yes, you can add multiple products to your cart before checkout.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: You can track your order from the Orders section of your account.

Q: What payment methods are accepted?
A: We accept UPI, Credit Card, Debit Card, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery where available.

Q: Can I save products for later?
A: Yes, you can add products to your wishlist if the feature is available.

Q: Is my personal information secure?
A: Yes, customer data is protected using secure authentication and encryption.
