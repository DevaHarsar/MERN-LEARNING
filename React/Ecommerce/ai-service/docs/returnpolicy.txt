Return Policy

Customers may request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.

The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

Products that are physically damaged by the customer cannot be returned.

Refunds are processed after the returned item passes inspection.

Digital products and gift cards are non-refundable.

Certain products may not be eligible for return due to hygiene or safety reasons.

The refund amount will be credited to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days after approval.
